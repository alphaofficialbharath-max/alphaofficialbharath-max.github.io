What each count means

Words - any stretch of visible letters or numbers separated by spaces, so "good-day" counts as one word and numbers count too.

Characters - every character you typed including spaces. Use it for forms and captions with strict limits.

Sentences - text up to and including full stops, question marks and exclamation marks; an unfinished final line still counts as one sentence.

Reading time - estimated at a calm 200 words per minute, the average for silent reading.

When a word counter saves marks

  • Exam answers and assignments with word limits you must not cross.
  • Essays, applications and question banks where the teacher sets a strict "500 words maximum".
  • Blog posts and captions where readers drop off after a certain reading time.
